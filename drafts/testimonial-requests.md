# Testimonial Request Templates

_4-6 ex-colleagues to ask. Goal: get 1-2 LinkedIn recommendations (Services-tagged) and 1-2 short text quotes for portfolio + Upwork (Upwork lets you import non-Upwork testimonials)._

---

## Who to ask (priority order)

1. **IACE direct manager** — knows the result-engine + leaderboard work, current role, freshest signal
2. **Masai data lead / engineering manager** — knows ETL consolidation, dashboards, the 80% query speedup
3. **Masai stakeholder (ops or curriculum)** — saw the Reporting Autopilot deliver weekly briefings
4. **Gamify founder/marketing lead** — saw WhatsApp + admissions automation + 10K+ student impact
5. **A peer engineer at IACE or Masai** — adds technical credibility (clean code, debugging, on-time)
6. **Bootcamp instructor at Masai** — backs the Construct Week project and learning trajectory

Pick 4-6 to message. Leave 1-2 in reserve in case nobody responds in week 1.

## TEMPLATE 1 — LinkedIn DM (warm, asks for a recommendation tied to Services)

> Hi {Name},
>
> Quick favor — I just transitioned to freelance Data & AI Automation work alongside my IACE role and started taking on outside clients via Upwork, Fiverr, and LinkedIn Services. The thing missing on my profile is a couple of recommendations from people who saw the actual work, and you're at the top of that list.
>
> Would you be willing to leave me a short LinkedIn recommendation (3-5 sentences max) about {one specific thing — see hooks below}? Even a few lines helps a lot — clients pre-screen recommendations before they even click my proposal.
>
> If easier, I can draft something you'd be comfortable putting your name to and you just edit / approve. No pressure if it's not the right time.
>
> Thanks either way 🙏
> Surya

**Hooks per person (pick the one that fits):**
- IACE: "the production result-generation engine and the multi-source data warehouse work"
- Masai (data lead): "the 40 → 20 ETL consolidation and the 80% query speedup"
- Masai (ops/curriculum): "the weekly briefings and dashboards I built that saved your team ~20 hours/week"
- Gamify: "the WhatsApp automation that helped land 10,000+ admissions"
- Peer engineer: "code quality / debugging / cross-team collaboration"
- Bootcamp instructor: "Construct Week project and trajectory through the program"

## Where each testimonial goes once you have it

| Source | LinkedIn rec? | Portfolio site? | Upwork testimonial? | Fiverr profile? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IACE manager | YES (Services-tagged) | YES | YES (import) | quote in About |
| Masai data lead | YES | YES | YES | — |
| Masai stakeholder | YES | YES | — | quote in About |
| Gamify | optional | YES | YES | — |
| Peer | optional | NO | — | — |
| Bootcamp | optional | NO | — | — |

Upwork lets you "Request a testimonial" via the Testimonials section — but it sends an email asking the person to write directly inside Upwork. Easier path: get the LinkedIn rec or text quote first, then **import** via Upwork's "Add testimonial" form (you fill in the person's name/role/company + their quote, no email required).

- **Day 0 (today):** Send 3 messages — IACE manager, Masai data lead, Gamify founder. Use Template 1 (LinkedIn DM).

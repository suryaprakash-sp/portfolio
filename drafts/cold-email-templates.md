# Cold Email Templates

Two scripts. One for EdTech CTO/Head of Engineering, one for SMB Ops/COO. Both end in the same CTA: free 30-min ops audit.

## Sending mechanics (user action)

- Send from `suryaprakash.manubolu@gmail.com` (the contact email, not OAuth one). 5–8 emails per day max — anything more triggers spam classifiers.
- Personalize the *first sentence* and the *case-study analog* line. The rest is template.
- Use BCC: yourself for tracking. Or use a free tier of Mixmax / Mailtrack for opens.
- Send Tue–Thu, 9–11 AM IST (8:30–10:30 AM India / 11 PM–1 AM PT — overnight delivery to US inboxes lands at the top of their morning).
- Don't send from a corporate domain you don't own. Gmail is fine for cold outreach when the email is short + zero attachments.
- **Never** attach the lead-magnet PDF — link to it. Attachments trigger filters.

## Anti-spam hygiene

- Subject lines under 50 chars, no $$$ / FREE / GUARANTEED / !!!
- One link maximum (Calendly OR portfolio, not both — pick one per email)
- No images, no HTML formatting — plain text only
- Plain `--` signature with name + role + portfolio URL
- Don't include `unsubscribe` (only required if you have a list — you're sending 1:1)

## TEMPLATE A — EdTech CTO / Head of Engineering

**Use when:** Recipient runs engineering at an EdTech company (LMS, coaching, certification, test-prep). Look for "Director of Engineering," "VP Engineering," "Head of Tech," "CTO" titles. Companies 50–500 employees.

**Subject options (pick 1):**
- `Quick question about your data pipeline`
- `<Company>'s exam result workflow — quick idea`
- `4 yrs in EdTech ops automation — would this fit <Company>?`

```

Either way, here are 10 production case studies of the same pattern: suryaprakash-sp.github.io/portfolio/lead-magnet.pdf
```

## Variant tweaks (A/B test)

- Subject line is the biggest lever. Test 2 subjects against each other for a week each.
- Test sending from name `Surya Manubolu` vs `Surya Prakash Manubolu` — shorter is friendlier in inbox.
- Test ending with the Calendly link vs ending with the portfolio link. Calendly converts higher when recipient is already curious; portfolio converts higher when they need credibility first.

## Follow-up cadence

- Day 0: Send.
- Day 5 (if no reply): One-line follow-up. `Hi [Name], floating this back up in case it got buried. Same offer stands. — Surya`
- Day 14: Final follow-up with a different angle — link a *new* case study or a *specific* observation about their company. If still silent, drop them.

Don't 4th follow-up. The line between persistent and annoying is 3 emails.

## Disqualifiers — do NOT email these

- Anyone who's posted publicly about hating cold emails (search Twitter for their name + "cold email" first)
- Series A+ companies whose CTO has 10K+ followers (they're inundated; reach out via warm intro instead)
- Companies your competition is already publicly serving (check LinkedIn "people you may know" who follow them)
- Anyone in your own EdTech network who already knows you (use LinkedIn DM instead — warmer)

# Cold Email Target Seed List

Starting list of 20 prospects (10 EdTech + 10 SMB ops). **Build your own list from here** — these are illustrative archetypes, not researched contacts. Spending 15 min on each prospect (LinkedIn / their site / a recent post) before emailing yields 5x the response rate of cold-blast.

## Research workflow per prospect (15 min each)

1. **Find** — Sales Navigator (free trial) or LinkedIn search by title + industry. Filter India-based for SMB B template, India + global for EdTech A template.
2. **Verify email** — hunter.io free tier (25/mo) or guess `firstname@company.com` and verify with verifier.email.
3. **Skim recent activity** — last 3 LinkedIn posts, last tweet, latest podcast/blog. The personalization line in the cold email comes from this.
4. **Save to your tracking spreadsheet** with: name, company, title, email, recent activity (1 line), date sent, date follow-up, status.

## Tracking sheet schema (Google Sheets)

| Date Sent | Name | Company | Title | Email | Personalization Hook | Template (A/B) | Subject | Status | Follow-up Date | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Status values: `Sent`, `Opened`, `Replied`, `Booked Audit`, `Disqualified`, `No Response`.

## Where to actually find names

1. **LinkedIn Sales Navigator free trial** (1 month) — best yield. Filter by industry + company size + title.
2. **Crunchbase** — find recently funded SMBs (cash flush + likely hiring help with ops).
3. **Indian VC newsletter "Startup Pulse" / "Inc42 / YourStory"** — recent funding announcements with founder names.
4. **Tracxn** — Indian startup database, free tier OK.
5. **Twitter / LinkedIn searches** for "we just hit X" / "ops nightmare" — these are pain signals.
6. **Reddit r/IndiaSmallBusiness** — founders complaining about ops in public.

## Red flags = skip

- Burned by LinkedIn DMs already (their feed has multiple "stop pitching me" posts)
- Hiring "AI engineer" full-time already (they don't need a freelancer)
- Series B+ funded (your sweet spot is bootstrapped or Seed/A — they need help, B+ has bigger budgets and other criteria)
- Already publicly endorsing a competitor (Zapier expert, OpenAI partner, Make.com case study)

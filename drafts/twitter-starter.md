## Cadence plan (after first 5)

- **Mon/Wed/Fri**: 1 tweet — usually a debugging anecdote, hot take, or build-in-public update
- **Tue**: Reply to 5 prominent AI/data folks' threads with substantive takes (not "great post!")
- **Sat**: 1 thread — deeper dive (4–8 tweets) on a technical lesson. Cross-link from LinkedIn newsletter.

## Threading templates

For technical deep-dives, follow this structure:

```
1/ Hook with a counterintuitive claim or a real number.
2/ The setup — what was happening / why it matters.
3/ The naive approach + why it failed.
4/ The actual approach + the key insight.
5/ Code/diagram (use carbon.now.sh screenshot for code).
6/ The result — measurable outcome.
7/ Generalize: the lesson + 1 question for the reader.
8/ Soft CTA: portfolio / lead-magnet / Calendly.
```

## What to NOT do

- Don't reply-guy every Anthropic tweet. Engage substantively or skip.
- Don't post motivational quotes. Audience hates it.
- Don't tag random influencers hoping for retweets. Earn engagement with substance.
- Don't shill the Fiverr gigs directly more than 1 in 10 tweets. Build authority first.
- Don't cross-post LinkedIn content verbatim — adapt for the platform.

## Metrics to watch (month 1)

- **Followers** — target 100 organic by end of month 1
- **Profile clicks** (Analytics tab) — target 5%+ of impressions
- **Calendly clicks** with `utm_source=twitter` (add to bio link)
- **Inbound DMs** with real questions — these are the warmest leads
